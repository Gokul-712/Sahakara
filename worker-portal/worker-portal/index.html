WHAT MAKES THIS A COOPERATIVE, NOT A MARKETPLACE

Built so the people doing the work keep the power.

01

Fee covers servers, not profit

No commission on your labour. The small platform fee only covers hosting and verification costs — nothing more.

02

A community that speaks for itself

Workers organise into local groups with an elected representative who reports concerns directly to the federation.

03

You can contest the algorithm

Job allocation runs on a transparent model — and workers can formally appeal or propose changes to it through governance.

04

Every worker, in their own language

The full worker experience — registration, jobs, payments, appeals — is available in your preferred language.
